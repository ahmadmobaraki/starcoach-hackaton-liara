# NOTICE

## Impeccable

`rules/design.md` distills ideas from [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable) by Paul Bakaus,
licensed under the Apache License 2.0. Read from version 4.3.1:
`reference/craft-floor.md`, `reference/new-work.md`, `reference/shape.md`, `reference/animate.md`
and `reference/document.md`.

These ideas were taken over:
- the direction round (candidates from the audience's own world, a roll that deals the lead, the
  canon card as the user's exit, safer and bolder re-rolls);
- the color strategies and the scene sentence that decides light or dark;
- the craft floor and the refuse list;
- the motion timing and purpose rules;
- the rule that `DESIGN.md` is written from the built result, in the DESIGN.md format.

They were rewritten in our own words, shortened and changed:
- adapted for Persian and RTL (glyph coverage, no letter-spacing or italics, logical Tailwind
  utilities, no `tabular-nums`);
- merged into a single file;
- partly enforced by `scripts/check.mjs` (contrast, RTL utilities).

No Impeccable file or script is included in this repository.

## product-ux-architect

Version 0.x of fordi was designed after reviewing a local archive of `product-ux-architect` 1.0.0,
which had no license file. No file from it was copied. What remains in 1.0 is the general idea of
separating facts, assumptions and decisions (`ux/idea.md`).
